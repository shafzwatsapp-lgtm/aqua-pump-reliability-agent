## Position it accurately

Call AQUA a **pump reliability investigation agent**. It demonstrates a repeatable decision-support process. Validated predictive maintenance and remaining-life modelling are future work.

The new front page explains the engineering user, three evidence sources, four-step workflow, interactive evidence revision and current implementation limits. The operations desk is at `/desk`.

## What keeps this from 5/5 scores

- All plant and maintenance inputs are simulated.
- Support scores and FMEA ratings are illustrative rules, not calibrated predictions.
- No independent labelled asset history or utility pilot validates performance.
- No live historian/Maximo integration demonstrates work across existing utility systems.
- Browser voice support is implemented but not fully validated across browsers.
- One earlier model answer was rejected by verification; later baseline, contrary-finding and sensor-quality runs passed.
- A convincing two-minute demonstration and complete public submission links still matter separately from the product.

Adding Hermes or more sponsor tools alone would not resolve these gaps.
